# Complex Numbers

Complex mode makes it possible to draw and manipulate numbers directly on the complex plane.

## Enabling complex mode

Open Graph Settings and turn on the Complex Mode switch.

<img className="desmos-article-image" src="/img/basic/en/complex/settings.png" alt="Enabling complex mode in Desmos" loading="lazy" />

## The complex plane

Entering a complex number automatically draws it as a point on the complex plane.

## Complex arithmetic

<DesmosExpression expressions={[
  '(1+i)+(2-2i)=3-i', '(1+i)(2-2i)=4',
  '\\mathrm{real}(2+4i)=2', '\\mathrm{imag}(2+4i)=4',
  '\\mathrm{conj}(2+4i)=2-4i', '\\mathrm{arg}(1+i)=\\frac{\\pi}{4}',

Desmos supports real and imaginary parts, conjugates, arguments, magnitudes, and Euler’s identity.
