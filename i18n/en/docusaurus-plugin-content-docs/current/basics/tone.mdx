# Playing Sound with `tone`

The `tone` function plays sound from the Desmos Graphing Calculator. Changing frequencies over time can even produce simple music.

## `tone(Frequency, Gain)`

$\mathrm{tone}(a)$ plays a sound at frequency $a\,[\mathrm{Hz}]$. Supported frequencies range from $20\,\mathrm{Hz}$ to $20000\,\mathrm{Hz}$.

<DesmosExpression expressions={['\\mathrm{tone}(440)']} />

A speaker button appears above the expression. Desmos starts muted, so press the button to hear the $440\,\mathrm{Hz}$ sine wave.

<img className="desmos-article-image" src="/img/basic/tone/tone.png" alt="The audio button above a tone expression" loading="lazy" />

The second argument controls gain.

<DesmosExpression expressions={['\\mathrm{tone}(440,1.5)']} />

Maximum gain depends on frequency and is calculated as $\min(10,660/\text{frequency})$.

## Beats

Combining two nearby frequencies creates a periodic change in loudness called a beat. Adjust the frequencies in this graph to see and hear the effect.

<DesmosEmbed id="olycwp0fzd" title="Exploring beats with tone" height={560} options={{expressions: true, settingsMenu: true, trace: true}} />

For frequencies $f_1$ and $f_2$, the beat frequency is

$$
|f_1-f_2|\,[\mathrm{Hz}].
$$
